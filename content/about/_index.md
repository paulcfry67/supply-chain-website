# About Supply Chain Daily

Supply Chain Daily provides comprehensive, data-driven analysis of supply chain issues affecting manufacturing and distribution sectors across the United States, with particular emphasis on automotive manufacturing.

## Our Mission

We deliver daily intelligence briefings to help supply chain professionals, manufacturers, and industry stakeholders make informed decisions in an increasingly complex global supply chain environment.

## What Sets Us Apart

- **Daily Updates**: Fresh analysis every business day
- **Industry Focus**: Deep expertise in automotive, electronics, and industrial equipment
- **Data-Driven**: Analysis based on real-time supply chain data and industry reports  
- **Actionable Intelligence**: Practical recommendations you can implement
- **Professional Analysis**: Content generated by AI systems trained on supply chain economics and logistics

## Coverage Areas

### Automotive Manufacturing
- OEM production challenges
- Tier 1/2/3 supplier issues  
- Semiconductor shortages
- Raw material availability

### Electronics Sector
- Component availability
- Manufacturing capacity
- Global sourcing challenges

### Industrial Equipment
- Heavy machinery supply chains
- Construction equipment
- Agricultural machinery
